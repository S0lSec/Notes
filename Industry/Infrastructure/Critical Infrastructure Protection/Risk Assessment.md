Risk assessment is the activity of identifying risks, such as:
- Natural disasters
- Cyber threats
- Equipment failure
- Human error
- etc
These risks are then categorised (high, medium, low) and mitigated.
## Risk Assessment Steps
1. Identify hazards and threats
	- Research asset
	- Find out features of the asset
	- Identify threats in the features of the asset
2. Identify assets
	- Physical
	- Human
	- Intellectual
3. Assess vulnerabilities
	- Find hazards and threats then identify security issues
4. Determine impact
	- What does it impact
5. Assess likelihood
	- Percentage of the impact happening
	- Evaluate based on:
		- Historical data
		- Industry trends
		- Expert judgement
6. Risk rating
	- Based on the impact and likelihood
	- Use a scale such as:
		- Low
		- Medium
		- High
## Mitigation Strategies
For each Risk identified a mitigation strategy must be developed.

To develop this strategy, for each risk identified list the measures to prevent or reduce the risk. Mitigation strategies can be found in the [Essential 8](https://www.cyber.gov.au/publication/essential-eight-explained)
### Steps
1. Identify risk treatments
	- Determine appropriate risk treatment
	- Such as:
		- Avoid
		- Mitigate
		- Transfer
		- Accept
2. Develop mitigation plans
	- If a risk is being mitigated develop a plan including steps to:
		- Reduce the risk
		- Resources required
		- Timeline
		- Responsible person or team
3. Implement mitigation plans
	- Implement and monitor plans
4. Review and update
	- Review effectiveness of the mitigation plans and update as required
## Protection Measures
**(Ensure protection measure fits within budget)**
1. Identify required measures
	- Physical security measures (fencing, locks)
	- Cybersecurity measures (firewalls, IDS)
	- Human resource measures (training, background checks)
2. Implement measures
	- Purchase equiptment
	- Install software
	- Conduct training
3. Test measures
	- Ensure measures are working as expected
	- This can involve:
		- Drills
		- Simulations
		- Real-world tests
4. Review and update
	- Regularly review and update protection measures based on testing results.
