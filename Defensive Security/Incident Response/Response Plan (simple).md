# Triage Metrics

|                    | **Not Significant** | **Minor** | **Moderate** | **Major** | **Severe** |
| ------------------ | ------------------- | --------- | ------------ | --------- | ---------- |
| **Almost Certain** | High                | Medium    | Very High    | High      | High       |
| **Likely**         | Low                 | Low       | High         | Very High | Low        |
| **Possible**       | Low                 | Low       | Very High    | High      | Very High  |
| **Unlikely**       | Medium              | Medium    | High         | Low       | Low        |
| **Rare**           | Low                 | Low       | Medium       | Low       | Very High  |
