The documentation process must include detailed information about stakeholders and digital devices.

All related aspects to the incident response process should be documented and filed for easy reference. It is suggested that the follwing elements are included:
- All system events (audit records)
- All actions taken (including the time that an action is performed)
- All external converations

An incident report document should include the following from the IR team at the end of an exercise:
- An explanation of the exact structure of events
- The method of discovery
- Preventive measures put in place

The objective of documentation is to identify possible areas of improvement in the incident handling and reporting procedures. (reviews, lessons learned should be documented)'

All incident must be logged to the incident handling log and assessment tools. A record of all action taken to remediate the incident, including chain of custody records and deviations must be included.

The incident response plan and procedures are reviews no less than annually and updates are tracked in the version history.

Plan review should include:
- Review supporting documents and forms listed in the supporting document List to ensure they are accurate and useful. 
- Review Appendices to ensure they are valid and valuable. 
- Review completed Incident Reporting Forms and corrective action plans for the recommended plan and procedure updates. 
- Compare recent changes to the organisation's infrastructure and management structure to documented plans and procedures.
