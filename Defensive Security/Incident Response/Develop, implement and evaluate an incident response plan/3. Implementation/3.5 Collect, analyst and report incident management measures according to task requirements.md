There are different metrics through which incidents can be managed, and the response is recorded meticulously.
1. Severity of the incident
2. Minutes of downtime
3. Meantime to resolution
## Containment
- Regain control by limiting damage
- Isolate compromised systems
- May interrupt business functions
- Evaluate the risk of continuing operations vs regaining control
- Minimise impact of business operations
## Eradication
- New OS should be reloaded into the compromised server after the investigation
- Enhance the security of the system
	- Apply recent patches
	- Disable pointless services
	- Install anti-virus
	- Apply the organisations security policy
