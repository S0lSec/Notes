Develop a systemic technique to detect, analyse and handle the incidents.
A sucessful incident response plan should promptly analyse the situation and validate the incident with accetable documenting methods.

Condition updates should contain the following
- Incident data and time
- Status of the incident
- Incident type and classification
- Scope
- Impact
- Severity
- Contact details for the incident manage and key incident response personnel

Situation updates should be prepared and given out to internal stakeholders at regular periods.

It is important to be active in enhancing and distributing your condition reports to reduce stakeholders need to approach you with various questions about the incident.
## Reporting
Reporting contains planning and submitting information resulting from the analysis phase.
It is required to report the outcomes of the analysis, such as explaining the actions or activies, what actions are yet to be performed, and the recommendations to improve the exisiting policy and procedure and find solutions to other aspects of the incident response plan.

Several factors affect reporting such as:
**Alternative Explanations**
The incident response team must use a meticulous approach to prove or disprove every possible proposed clarification.

**Audience Consideration**
Understand the audience.
An incident involving law enforcement association requires extemely comprehensive details of all information collected and may need copies of the complete evidentiary data attained.

**Actionable data**
Reporting comprises finding actionable information collcted from data that may allow the incident response team to gather new information sources.

Whn associated with the reporting process, the incident response team should identify any issues that may need to be resolved, such as policy flaws or procedural inaccuracies.

The incident response team should constantly review their process and practices.
