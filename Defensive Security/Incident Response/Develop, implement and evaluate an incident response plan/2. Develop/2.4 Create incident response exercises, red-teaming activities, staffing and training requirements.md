Incident response activities help in creating scenarios to test and identify potential threats.
## Incident Response Exercises
- Provides a better understanding and awareness of threats
- Helps evaluate overall incident preparedness
- Identified inequalities in the IR plan
- Improves response times

IR exercises also underline how well an organisation meets it IR performance goals.
## Some useful incident response exercises
- Simulated incidents to prepare staff and check system functionality 
- Scenario questions and determine the most likely response 
- Conducting interviews and conferences to keep a check 
- Media reports and timely newsletters can help in up-to-date knowledge 
- Conducting training sessions and discussing incidents.
## Assess current resources
Organisations must evaluate how efficiently staff handles the available IR tools and resources.

Resources that should be evaluated during the IR exercise are:
- If management is a member of the Incident Response Team, they typically include someone from management that offers leadership, an individual from information systems security to find the source of the issue, along with IT staff 
- Accessibility of IR training resources. 
- Continuous monitoring systems to [uncover incidents](https://www.rsisecurity.com/services/managed-detection-response/) 
- Reliable backups and readiness of forensics tools 
- Storerooms for documents that contain policies, recording, reporting, and archiving vital information 
- Accessibility and security of communications in-house and outside the system
## Get Feedback on needed security upgrades
Specific areas that organisations commonly discover need improvements are:
- Was the offered exercise scenario relevant to the company's present security posture? 
- Did the staff comprehend why specific employees were instructed to take part in the exercise? 
- Is the response team acquainted with the IR plan and accomplished implementing it? 
- Can the IR team manage a crisis and adapt to altering scenarios while using the available tools and resources? 
- Do different team members communicate appropriately? 
- Is information supplied to the correct personnel on time?
## Improve Decision Response Time
Red teaming can improve the time required to respond to an incident.

Benefits of red teaming activities:
- Expose the vulnerabilities and risks involving technology, people, and physical platforms
- It helps the organisation to be competitive and maintaining epitome security
- Getting you prepared and equipped to deal with real threats
- The organisation can test their efficiency and find their weakness

It is advisable to create threat vectors to know the weaknesses, such as getting access to essential security systems (username and passwords). Next would be hampering the network security and finding the red flags.

Effective scenario generation:
- Finding the possible threat factors to your organisation
- Evaluating the organisation situation in terms of awareness and applications
- Ensuring adequate staff functionality
- Assessing risks and critical assets
