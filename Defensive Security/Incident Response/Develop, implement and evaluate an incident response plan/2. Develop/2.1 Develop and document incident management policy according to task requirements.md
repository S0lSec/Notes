The reason for creating policies is to ensure consistency and enhance productivity; therefore, developing a useful approach that includes all the significant factors such as organisations management, identifying the risks and orderly governance.
## Incident Response Policy
- Defines the incidents and their consequences.
- Provides an organisational structure and delineation of roles, responsibilities and levels of authority

Organisations create and maintain procedures and guidelines for performing tasks based on their policies and applicable laws and regulations after an incident. Prioritisation or severity ratings of incidents can also be used as a guide.

Policies and procedures explain the forensic actions that an organisation should or should not take depending on the circumstances. This includes describing the necessary safeguards for sensitive information that forensic tools might capture.

A policy is created to define the roles and responsibilities of adhering to the laws.
## Policy Elements
The incident response policy the the basis of the incident response program. Describing what events are consider incidents, determining the organisational structure for incident response, identifies roles and responsibilities and records reporting incidents' requirements. (Policies will be different for each organisation)

Policies key elements:
- Declaration of management approval 
- Aim and purposes of the IR policy 
- Scope of the policy as per the organisation structure (to whom and what it applies and under what situations) 
- Description of security incidents and associated expressions 
- Organisational structure and classification of roles, responsibilities, and authority 
- Must include the authority of the IR team to remove or disable equipment 
- To monitor dubious activity, the necessary conditions for reporting certain kinds of incidents 
- Guidelines for external communications and information sharing  
- Management process 
- Prioritisation or severity ratings of incidents 
- Performance measures  
- Reporting and contact forms
