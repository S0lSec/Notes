## Incident Handling Stages
**Preparation**
- Contact information
- Communication plan
- Encryption software
- War room
- Security evidence/sensitive information

**Implementation and Design**
- Select/purchase systems 
- Implement policy/procedures 
- Establish logs/auditing 
- Establish service/SLAs

**Detection and Analysis**
- Identify signatures 
- Anomalies 
- Events 
- Errors/Alerts

**Containment, Eradication and Recovery**
- Identify Access Channels 
- Deny/Repeat Access 
- Remove malicious code

**Post-Incident requirements**
- Data collection 
- Post-event analysis 
- Continuous improvement
## Incident handling and communication plan
- Creating jump kits that have computing devices and essential software's to assist in risk containment. 
- To perform simulated incidents to test staff and system configurations. 
- Recording team members contact details, including primary and secondary contact details 
- Adequate escalation information

## Reporting requirements
Vary among organisations but are usually notified as:
- Leader of information security 
- The provincial information security manager 
- Incident response teams within the organisation 
- External incident response teams (if appropriate) 
- System owner 
- Human resources (for cases involving employees, such as harassment through email) 
- Public affairs (incidents that might produce publicity) 
- Legal department (for incidents that are likely legal implications) 
- Law enforcement (if applicable)

During incident handling, the IR team might require to provide status updates to certain parties, even the entire organisation. 

The team must plan and prepare numerous communication methods. such as out-of-band methods and choose suitable methods for a specific incident.
## Viable communication methods
- Email 
- Website (internal, external, or portal) 
- Telephone calls or video conferences (e.g., skype, zoom) 
- In-person (e.g., daily sessions) 
- Voice mail greeting (e.g., set up a distinct voice mailbox for incident updates 
- Greeting message to indicate the present incident status 
- Paper (e.g., put up notices on newsletter boards, hand out notices at all entrance points)
