Feedback plays an essential role in developing an effective IR plan. So it is vital to submit the plan for assessment to adequate authorities to create real-time insights for further improvements.

Some key elements that are looked at upon review:
- Quantifying the errors
- Meeting the organisations standards
- Adhering to the standard policies
- Receiving feedback from relevant stakeholders and incident response team
## Incident Response Personnel
There must be a team manager and one or more deputy managers who presume authority in the managers absence.

The team manager does a range of tasks, such as acting as a liaison with top management and additional teams and organisations, resolving crises and ensures that the IR team has the essential personnel, resources and skills. (Some teams may have technical managers)

Incident response team members should have outstanding technical skills, for instance system and network administration, software development, technical support or intrusion detection. Every team member must adopt design thinking along with problem-solving skills and critical thinking abilities.

## Incident Response Plan Review
The organisation will review and assess the organisations current policies, processes and procedures, checklist and workflow documentation related to incident response plans. The IR plan reviews objective is to identify gaps in existing plans, such as roles and responsibilities of IR stakeholders, escalation and communication processes, incident handling coordination procedures and other functions critical to executing an IR process.

The information that will be included in the review will be grouped into the following categories: 
- Planning and preparation 
- Detection and classification 
- Collection and analysis 
- Containment and eradication 
- Remediation and recovery  
- Assessment and reporting

## Responding to feedback and develop an action plan
- Resist, React, reject: this response can wear down trust and undervalue feedback and, in turn, sets the prospects around what the team can and can't discuss 
- Receive, Reflect, respond:  the individual takes the time they need to reflect on what they heard and respond accordingly
