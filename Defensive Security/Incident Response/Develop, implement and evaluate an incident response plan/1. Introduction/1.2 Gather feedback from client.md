## Incident response team
- Individuals who prepare and respond to an incident within an organisation
- Must be available when an incident has been suspected
- Primary goal is to identify and classify the attack to further measures can be taken to minimise data exposure.
## Purpose of IR team
A IR team is comprised of key members with each having their own speciality. They are equipped to detect, contain and mitigate incidents effectively.

They help keep the organisations security systems uptight and deal with incidents outside your control by reducing the risk factor to a minimum. 

The IR team usually works with the senior executive management team responsible for stakeholder engagement and communication, providing adequate resources, and fulfilling their capability demands.

**Incident response manager** is responsible for coordinating the team and minimising the threat and ensuring a speedy recovery. The manager will also undertake significant decisions regarding prioritising action plans, analysis and containing the incident.

**Security analyst** will be responsible for rectifying the organisations security systems flaws and finding fitting solutions.

**Lead investigators** responsibility is to find the root of the incident, investigate the evidence and aids in the system recovery.

**Threat researcher** will be responsible for undertaking contextual information regarding the incident and record the previous incidents and analyse them.

**Communication manager** will handle internal and external communication, including staff, management, stakeholders, legal authorities, customers and media.

**Legal or HR representative** are only required if there is a chance of the incident becoming a criminal matter.
## The goal of the incident response team
The IR teams primary purpose is to coordinate with team members and resources to minimise the impact and quickly restore operations.

Focusing on the following parameters:
1. **Analysis**: Document the extend, priority and breach to find the impacted areas or assets
2. **Reporting**: Perform escalations and report procedures
3. **Response**: Explore the root cause and develop strategies to recover and keep record
## Incident response team structure
They can be a group of thoroughly prepared individuals to respond to an incident in the organisation. The below chart depicts information about various structures that can be implemented to structure an incident response team. 

Within the incident response team, there can be divisions concerning the roles of dedicated members. Example: 

| Supervisory Team  | On-site team     | Support team        |
| ----------------- | ---------------- | ------------------- |
| Senior management | IT               | Human Resource (HR) |
| Security team     | General Security | Public affairs      |
| IT Auditors       | IT Auditors      | Legal               |
| Financial auditor |                  |                     |
Fundamental responsibility of the Supervisory Team includes:
- decision making and assessing steps taken to resolve matters
- communicating and interpreting technical information to senior management
- supervising and reviewing test findings based on the tests performed to verify the feasibility and efficiency of the incident response process
- collaborating with and supplying information to the IT support team 
- coordinating resources, e.g., software and hardware procurement, if required 
- maintaining systematic records of events and actions taken 
- attending, establishing, and conducting training for appropriate personnel 
- performing test scenarios and identifying corrective actions for each scenario 
- managing and reviewing the update, including lessons learned

The responsibility of the Site Team includes: 
- surveying and securing the systems and environment 
- containing the incident 
- eliminating incident and executing recovery procedures 
- collecting "recovery kit," e.g., recovery methods, contact list, hardware and software, tools, and soon 
- conducting training for staff 
- Updating and preserving the Incident Response Procedures 
    
The responsibility of the Support Team includes:
- providing logistic and technical support to other IR teams  
- updating public and relevant authorities via the media, websites, telephone, and others 
- coordinating communication with external parties

**Central Incident response team***: they are the single team that handles incidents throughout the organisation. This model is usually adequate for small businesses with limited geographical diversity in terms of computing services. 

**Distributed Incident Response Teams**: The organisation has numerous incident response teams responsible for an absolute logical or physical division. This model is valuable for large organisations (e.g., one team per division) and organisations with primary computing resources at distant locations. 

**In-house coordinating team**: An incident response team provides advice to other groups without having authority over those teams. 

**Contractors**: The organisation subcontracts its incident response work, usually to an onsite contractor. This model will most likely be used when the organisation requires a full-time, onsite incident response team but does not have sufficient available, qualified employees.
