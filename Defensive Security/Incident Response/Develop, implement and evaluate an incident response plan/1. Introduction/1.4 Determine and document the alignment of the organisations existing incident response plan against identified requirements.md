## Improvement
Things can always be improved, compliance, technical updates, documentation requirements and the impact of change on stakeholders. Always relying on existing systems can make your security outdated.

The team must beware of existing systems loopholes, and the group should work to get the best possible solutions to eliminate them.
#### Management
Determines IR policy, budget and staffing. Eventually, management is help responsible for the coordination between the various stakeholders.
#### Information Assurance
The information security team may be required through certain stages of incident handling such as prevention, containment, eradication and recovery.
#### IT Support
IT technical professionals such as network and system administrators have the required skills and best insight into the technology. This knowledge can confirm that the appropriate actions are taken for the impacted system.
#### Legal Department
They should review IR plans, procedures and policies to ensure their accordance with the law and Federal guidance, as well as the right to privacy.
#### Human Resources
If any staff member is suspected of causing an incident, the human resources may be engaged, for instance in helping with disciplinary action.
## Business Continuity Planning
BCP makes it essential that the documentation is aligned with the Incident Response Plan.

Some following factors that could aid are:
- Developing a risk profile for company assets 
- Instilling collaboration among the various stakeholders 
- Ensuring communications when cyber incidents occur 
- Coordinating Cyber Incident Response Plan with other plans 
- Facilitating a quick, viable recovery of mission-critical functions
## Physical Security and Facilities Management
IR team may require access to facilities throughout incident handling, for instance to acquire a compromised workstation from a sealed office.

Validate the alignment of the plan with organisations requirements. To validate plans, policy, agreements and procedures, clarify roles and responsibilities, and identify resource gaps in an organisations environment.
## Incident Documentation
When the IR team suspects an incident has taken place they should instantly record all the facts regarding the incident. A logbook is a useful and straightforward medium, but laptops, audio recorders and digital cameras can also be used.

Documenting system events, conversations and noticed variations that have taken place straight in the files might lead to a more effective, systematic and less error-prone handling of an issue.

The issue tracking system should include data as: 
- The up-to-date status of the incident (new, progress, forwarded for investigation, resolved, etc.)  
- An outline of the incident 
- Relevant indicators to the incident and other incidents related to this incident  
- The IT team takes actions  
- Maintain chain of custody   
- Impact reviews associated with the incident 
- Contact details for other involved parties (e.g., system owners, system administrators)  
- A list of evidence collected 
- Comments from the incident response team 
- Subsequent actions to be undertaken (e.g., rebuild the host, upgrade an application)
