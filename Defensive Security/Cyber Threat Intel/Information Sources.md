# Network Intelligence Communities
- **SANS**  - Internet storm center, popular early warning system
- **Mitre** - Maintains a list of CVE
- **FIRST** - Brings together a variety of computer security IR teams to foster cooperation and coordination in information sharing, incident prevention and rapid reaction
- **SecurityNewsWire** - Latest breaking news, pertaining to alerts, exploits and vulns
- **(ISC)2** - Vendor neutral education products and career services
- **CIS** - Focal point of cyber threat prevention, protection, response and recovery for governments.
