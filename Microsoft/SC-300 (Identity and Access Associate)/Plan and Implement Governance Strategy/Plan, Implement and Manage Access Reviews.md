# Plan for Access Review
Access reviews help ensure that users only have access to what they are suppose to have access to.  They are done in Entra ID Governance.

Pilot access reviews with a small group and target noncritical resources. Consider the following:
- What resources to review
- Who will review
- Test access
- Adjust, then test again
### Components of an Access Review Policy
- What resources must be reviewed?
- Whose access is being reviewed?
- How often should the review occur?
- Who'll perform the review?
- How will they no notified to do the review?
- What are the timelines to be enforced for the review?
- What automatic actions should be enforced based on the review?
- What happens if the reviewer doesn't respond in time?
- What manual actions will be taken based on the review results?
- What communications should be sent based on the actions taken?
### Establish a Plan for Access Review
- Review access packages
- Review group and apps
- Review Microsoft Entra roles
- Review Azure resource roles
### Plan Communications
- Comunicate changes in accountability
- Customise email communication:
	- Include personal message to reviewers
	- Include a hyprlink or reference to internal information on what the expectations of the review are
	- Include a link to instructions on how to perform a self-review of access
