# Benefits of Data Governance
- **For organization-wide data consumers:**
	- Data discovery - helps you easily find the data you need.
	- Secure access - facilitates safe access to your data.
	- Data understanding - providing what you need to know about the data.
	
- **For data owners and stewards:**
	- Data curation and management - helps you deliver high quality data that's easy to understand and safely access for organization-wide applications.
	- Responsible data use - helps you ensure that your data is used by intended users for intended purposes.
	- Impact analysis - understand actions on the data that may impact your data.
	
- **For data officers and CxO stakeholders:**
	- Data value creation - maximize value creation from your data while reducing operations spend.
	- Data estate standardization - create common controls across your data estate with federated accountability so your data is healthy and safe.
# Unified Catalog
Helps with data governance
## Governance Domains
(Group)
Used to organise your data estate through business concepts such as marketing and finance.
Used to provide context for your data
## Data Products
(Item)
A data product is a business contruct with a name, description, owners and a list of associated data assets.

A governance domain can hold many data products but a data product is managed by a single governance domain.
## Glossary Terms
(Description)
Provides context to your data assets and applies policies that dertermine how your data should be managed, governed and made discoverable for use.

Are helpful for organisation.
## Critical Data Elements
(Important Group)
CDEs are a logical grouping of important information. Data quality rules and access policies can be attached to these elements to further secure sensitive informaiton.
## OKRs
Objective and key results are trackable business objects tied to governance domains and data products to emphasise the value of business data.
## Access Policies
Allow you to manage access to your data and set up a system to provide access to requesting users.
## Search and Browse
Allows users to search data to easily find data required.
## Health Management
Allows your team to analyse and track your journy to complete data governance by monitoring your governance health, and using the provided health controls to track your progress.

Provides:
- Improved data quality
- Enhanced security
- Regulatory compliance
- Operational efficiency
## Data Quality
Empowers governance domain and data owners to assess and oversee the quality of their data ecosystem, facilitating targeting actions for improvement.
