Microsoft service trust portal is a site used to manage cloud data security and compliance for your organisation
# Microsoft's Privacy Principles
1. **Control** - Your in control of your privacy
2. **Transparency** - Transparent about data collection and use
3. **Security** - Protecting data via encryption
4. **Strong legal protections** - Obligating privacy laws
5. **No content-based targeting** - Not using email, chat, files, etc
6. **Benefits you** - Collects data to benefit you
