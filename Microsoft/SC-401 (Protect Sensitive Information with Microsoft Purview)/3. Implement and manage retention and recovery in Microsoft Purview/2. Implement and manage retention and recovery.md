# Auto-apply retention labels
Auto-apply retention labels assign labels based on content conditions that can be tested using simulation mode. **Labels must be published before they take effect**

Auto-apply automatically applies labels when content meets conditions such as sensitive info types, keywords, classifiers or cloud attachments.
# Understand policy and label precedence
Items can have policies, labels or both, if this is the case the most protective rule based on principle is applied.

- Retention takes priority over deletion
- The longest period is used
- Labels override policies for deletion
