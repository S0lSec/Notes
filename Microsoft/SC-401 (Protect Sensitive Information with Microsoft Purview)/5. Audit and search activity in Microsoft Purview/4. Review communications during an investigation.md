# Communication Compliance
Helps to identify and review messages that may violate policies or communication standards and support investigations when message content needs review.

Provides insight into communication content when needed for review.
### Use it to
- Identify messages that may require review
- Understand why an item was flagged
- Provide communication context for an investigation
- Support consistent review workflow

Gives reviewers tools to assess flagged communications as part of an investigation.
**Reviewers can**:
- View the message and surrounding context
- See why the item was flagged
- Add notes
- Mark the item as resolved or escalate it for further review
