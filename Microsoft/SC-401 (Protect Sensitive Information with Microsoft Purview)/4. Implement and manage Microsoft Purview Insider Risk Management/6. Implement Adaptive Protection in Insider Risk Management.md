# Adaptive Protection
Adaptive protection in Purview automatically adjusts security controls based on user risk.
High-risk users receive stronger protections while low-risk users retain normal productivity.

Integrates with:
- Data lifecycle management
- Conditional access
- DLP policies
## Risk Levels
Adaptive protection updates risk levels based on recent user activity and insider risk policies. You can customise how risk levels are assigned and how long each level stays active.
