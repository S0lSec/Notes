Alert generation matters for investigation because it:
- Shows what triggered the alert
- Clarifies which activities were evaluated
- Informs next steps
- Aligns alerts with policy criteria
# Manager alert volume
## To few alerts
- Enable more indicators
- Increase the number of users included in policy scope
- Lower trigger and indicator thresholds
## To many alerts
- Use analytics to identify high-risk areas
- Adjust threshold and include higher-value indicators
- Prioritse the most relevant content within policies
- Enable inline alert cusomtisation to refine policy sensitivity
