Alert generation matters for investigation because it:
- Shows what triggered the alert
- Clarifies which activities were evaluated
- Informs next steps
- Aligns alerts with policy criteria
# Manager alert volume
## To few alerts
- Enable more indicators
- Increase the number of users included in policy scope
- Lower trigger and indicator thresholds
## To many alerts
- Use analytics to identify high-risk areas
- Adjust threshold and include higher-value indicators
- Prioritise the most relevant content within policies
- Enable inline alert customisation to refine policy sensitivity
# Risk factors when analysing alerts
- **User risk factor**: Shows the triggering event and recent alert history to help understand user activity
- **Activity risk factor**: Lists detected actions for the alert, including both contributing and contextual activity
- **Asset risk factor**: Highlights content indicators such as labels, sensitive info detections or classifier matches
